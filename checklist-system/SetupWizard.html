Step 2 of 2: Build the forms

Three clicks, in this order, all in the Checklists menu:

  1. Validate sheet. Fix anything it lists as blocking. Warnings are OK.
  2. Rebuild forms now. Creates one Google Form per active checklist. The first time takes a few minutes.
  3. Install / repair triggers. Turns on the 15-minute check that rebuilds forms each morning and tracks what's late.

Then Checklists → Show form links gives you the links to post at each station.
