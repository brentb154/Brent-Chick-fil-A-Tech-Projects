# Chick-fil-A Restaurant Tools

Free operational tools for Chick-fil-A restaurants. The Google Sheets tools start as a **blank copy** that belongs only to you; the no-setup tools are single web pages that keep your data in your own browser. You never see another store's data, and you never have to touch code.

**Website:** [brentb154.github.io/Brent-Chick-fil-A-Tech-Projects](https://brentb154.github.io/Brent-Chick-fil-A-Tech-Projects/)

## Tools

| Tool | What it does | Get started |
|---|---|---|
| [Catering Quote Tool](catering-quote-tool/) | Build, email and print catering quotes in a couple of minutes. Tracks POs, tax-exempt certificates, delivery days and follow-ups. | [Setup guide](catering-quote-tool/SETUP_GUIDE.md) |
| [Training Tracker](training-tracker/) | Trainers log shifts on a Google Form; the sheet tracks hours by position, builds timelines and emails alerts when someone is inactive or ready to certify. | [Setup guide](training-tracker/SETUP_GUIDE.md) |
| [H.E.A.R.D. Log](guest-recovery-log/) | Passcode-protected guest-recovery log for a manager tablet. Flags repeat guests, shows trends, and emails alerts. | [Setup guide](guest-recovery-log/SETUP_GUIDE.md) |
| [Schedule Counter (Sheets)](schedule-counter-sheets/) | Staffing by the hour against real sales synced weekly from CFA Analytics, with a next-week forecast and reminders. | [Setup guide](schedule-counter-sheets/SETUP_GUIDE.md) |
| [Schedule Counter (No-Setup Version)](schedule-counter/) | Staff per hour from a HotSchedules report next to your sales forecast, with where to cut or add. Opens in your browser. | [Setup guide](schedule-counter/SETUP_GUIDE.md) |
| [Inventory Analyzer](inventory-analyzer/) | Flags end-of-month inventory jumps, zeros and missing items against recent months. Opens in your browser. | [Setup guide](inventory-analyzer/SETUP_GUIDE.md) |
| [Facilities Repair Tracker](facilities-tracker/) | Logs in-house repairs against contractor cost to show savings. Opens in your browser. | [Setup guide](facilities-tracker/SETUP_GUIDE.md) |
| [Productivity Tracker](productivity-tracker/) | Monthly productivity chart with a before/after comparison from the month you took over. Opens in your browser. | [Setup guide](productivity-tracker/SETUP_GUIDE.md) |

More tools are added here once their setup has been tested from scratch by someone who didn't build them.

## How the Google Sheets tools work

1. **Make a copy.** Each setup guide starts with a "Make a copy" link. You get a blank sheet with the tool attached, in your own Google account.
2. **Run the setup.** A menu in the sheet asks for your store's details once, then builds everything.
3. **Use it.** Your data stays in your copy. Nobody else, including whoever built the tool, can see it.
