Catering Quote Tool

Build, email and print catering quotes in a couple of minutes, with consistent pricing and a clean PDF every time. It runs in a Google Sheet you own, as a web app your catering team bookmarks.

➡️ To set it up, follow the Setup Guide. It takes about 20 minutes, with no code to touch.

What it does

  • Quotes in minutes: a searchable menu picker, pickup or delivery pricing, a one-click PDF, and email straight from the app
  • One store or two: run catering from one location or switch between two
  • PO tracking: missing-PO reminders before the event
  • Tax-exempt certificates: guests upload a PDF or photo with no login, from an emailed link or a QR code at the manager stand
  • Calendar and delivery days: orders go on Google Calendar, with a delivery runsheet and driver assignment
  • Follow-ups: optional reminder emails for quotes that haven't been answered
  • Supplies: turns the day's orders into what to pull and prep

More

  • How it works covers the tabs, the automations and the design decisions.
  • Every setting lives in the sheet's Settings tab and the app's Settings page. Nothing needs code changes.
