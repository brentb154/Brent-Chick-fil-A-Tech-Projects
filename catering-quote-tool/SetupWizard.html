Step 2 of 2: Publish the app

Already published before? You can skip this step. Your team link is under Deploy → Manage deployments.

First time? This turns the sheet into the catering app your team uses:

  1. In the menu at the top, click Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Click the blue Deploy button (top right) → New deployment.
  3. Click the ⚙️ gear next to "Select type" → Web app.
  4. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone.
  5. Click Deploy. If Google asks, click Authorize access and Allow.
  6. Copy the Web app URL and add ?view=app to the end. That's your team's link. Bookmark it.

"Anyone" lets guests upload tax-exempt forms without a Google login. Your team's link only works with ?view=app on the end, so don't post it publicly.
