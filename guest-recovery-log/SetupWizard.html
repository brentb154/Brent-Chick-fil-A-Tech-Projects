Step 2 of 2: Publish the app

Already published before? You can skip this step. Passcode changes take effect right away, and your team's link is under Deploy → Manage deployments.

First time? This turns the sheet into the app your managers use:

  1. In the menu at the top, click Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Click the blue Deploy button (top right) → New deployment.
  3. Click the ⚙️ gear next to "Select type" → Web app.
  4. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone.
  5. Click Deploy. If Google asks, click Authorize access and Allow.
  6. Copy the Web app URL. That's your team's link. Bookmark it on the manager tablet.

"Anyone" only means the page opens without a Google login. Nothing shows until someone types the login passcode.
